Isolated now has containers: separate spaces with their own files and internet, for apps you want to keep apart from your PC.

**Already have Isolated?** It updates itself: open the window and choose **Update now**, or run `isolated update`. New users: download `isolated.exe` below, open it, and choose **Settings > Install for this user**.

### New: containers
- Open the window's **Containers** tab (it was **Installed apps**) and choose **New container...**. Give it a name, such as `AI apps`, and choose whether its apps may use the internet (allowed by default).
- Put apps in it: **Install an app here...** for installers, **Run a program...** for portable apps you downloaded, or **Command prompt** to set tools up from a terminal inside the container.
- Everything those apps save, download or create stays in the container's own Documents, Downloads, Desktop and Programs folders, never in yours. They still cannot see your personal files.
- **Open files** lets you look around inside the container: copy what you made to your PC with **Copy to PC...**, drop files on it to add them, and start programs with **Run here**.
- The **Internet** switch on each container allows or blocks the network for its apps.
- Deleting a container removes it and everything in it.
- Command line: `isolated container create|run|shell|files|net|delete ...` (the same as `isolated box ...`).

### Known limits
- A container uses Windows' app sandbox, so apps that need a driver, a service or administrator rights still cannot run in one.
- Releases are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run.

Verify the download: the SHA-256 is in `isolated.exe.sha256`.
