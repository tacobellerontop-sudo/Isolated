Fixes Unity games, and other apps that keep their data in AppData\Local or AppData\LocalLow, stopping right after their splash screen inside Isolated.

**Already have Isolated?** It updates itself: open the window and choose **Update now**, or run `isolated update`. New users: download `isolated.exe` below, open it, and choose **Settings > Install for this user**.

### Fixed
- Inside a sandbox, Windows could not hand apps their LocalAppData and LocalLow folders, so apps that save there at startup quit. Unity games are the common case: they show the splash screen and then close. Isolated now gives each sandbox its own copies of those folders, kept with the sandbox's other private files, so the game's settings and saves stay out of your real AppData.

Verify the download: the SHA-256 is in `isolated.exe.sha256`.
